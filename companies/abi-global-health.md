# Abi Global Health

Abi Global Health provides a telemedicine platform that embeds health navigation and text, voice and video consultations into partner services.

- **Category:** Digital health and telemedicine
- **Website:** [Website](https://www.abiglobalhealth.com/)
- **Jobs / careers:** [Careers](https://apply.workable.com/abi-hiring/)
- **Historical remote signal:** Europe / EMEA remote signal

The official Workable board checked on 7 November 2026 lists remote medical opportunities in Ireland and Switzerland. The Swiss telehealth doctor posting describes flexible, question-based remote consultations with no minimum workload commitment. These signals concern the clinical professional network, not a company-wide remote policy for corporate or technology staff.

## Verification caveat

The Swiss posting requires an active Swiss cantonal medical licence, completed residency, a HIN account or willingness to obtain one, and English and German fluency. Country, licensing and engagement terms vary by role; flexible clinical work is not proof of salaried remote employment across Europe. The same board lists technology submissions as on-site in Barcelona or hybrid in Dublin. No Spain-remote or unrestricted cross-border policy is established.
