# Qonto

Qonto provides business accounts, payments and financial management tools for small and medium-sized businesses.

- **Category:** Financial technology
- **Website:** [Website](https://qonto.com/)
- **Jobs / careers:** [Careers](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/qonto)
- **Historical remote signal:** Spain remote signal

Qonto’s default model is three office days and up to two remote days weekly, plus 12 bonus remote days within +/-3 hours of CET. Explicit fully remote exceptions exist: the current Staff Software Engineer, AI Agents (Python) role authorizes remote work within France, Germany, Spain or Italy. The official Ashby feed also lists Spain remote engineering roles. Eligibility is limited to the role’s named hiring countries, not all Europe.
