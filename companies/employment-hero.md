# Employment Hero

Employment Hero provides HR, payroll and employment-management software; Humi is its Canadian platform.

- **Category:** HR and payroll technology
- **Website:** [Website](https://employmenthero.com/)
- **Jobs / careers:** [Careers](https://employmenthero.com/careers/)
- **Historical remote signal:** Hybrid or country-restricted evidence

Humi’s careers URL now redirects to Employment Hero. The current careers policy explicitly combines work from anywhere with attracting top talent globally, supporting a worldwide company watch. Approved country/role, work rights and employment terms must still be checked; do not treat Canadian Humi roles as automatically Spain-eligible.

- **Historical record reviewed:** 2026-11-11
- **Public-source research date:** 2026-11-11

## Verification caveat

Eligibility is role- and country-specific. Policy, talent-pool and empty-board evidence is not a guarantee of a current vacancy or of unrestricted cross-border employment. The original upstream interview-process claim has not been independently certified.
