# Mattermost

Mattermost develops secure, open-core collaboration software for organizations that need control over their communications and operational workflows.

- **Category:** Secure collaboration software
- **Website:** [Website](https://mattermost.com/)
- **Jobs / careers:** [Careers](https://mattermost.com/company/careers/)
- **Historical remote signal:** Hybrid or country-restricted evidence

Confirmed through an explicit company hiring policy, not a current European vacancy: the remote-first handbook says the company hires in Germany and the UK, as well as the US and Canada, and uses an employer of record outside its entity locations. Its current Greenhouse API returned 11 roles, located in the US, Canada and Japan; none explicitly offered European or Spanish eligibility. Most roles require availability for video calls between 8am and 1pm California time, with role-specific exceptions. Team meetups occur. Employment is tied to the contractual country and relocation needs written approval; EOR availability is not permission to hire in every country.

- **Historical record reviewed:** 2026-11-11
- **Public-source research date:** 2026-11-11

## Verification caveat

Eligibility is role- and country-specific. Policy, talent-pool and empty-board evidence is not a guarantee of a current vacancy or of unrestricted cross-border employment. The original upstream interview-process claim has not been independently certified.
