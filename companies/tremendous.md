# Tremendous

Tremendous provides a business platform for sending bulk payouts, gift cards and rewards through a dashboard, integrations and APIs.

- **Category:** Payments and rewards software
- **Website:** [Website](https://www.tremendous.com/)
- **Jobs / careers:** [Careers](https://www.tremendous.com/careers/)
- **Historical remote signal:** Hybrid or country-restricted evidence

The official careers page describes a fully remote company. The live Senior Customer Success Manager, International posting explicitly permits remote work from Spain or the UK, with residence in one of those countries and regular overlap with US colleagues. These countries are role-specific evidence, not a guarantee that all roles hire there; several engineering and product postings are restricted to the Americas. Careers describes semi-annual company offsites and in-person team meetings; mandatory attendance, duration and travel-cost arrangements are not specified.
