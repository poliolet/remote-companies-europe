# Toptal

Toptal operates a talent network connecting businesses with freelance specialists in software development, design, marketing, management consulting, and project delivery.

- **Category:** Talent marketplace
- **Website:** [Website](https://www.toptal.com/)
- **Jobs / careers:** [Careers](https://www.toptal.com/careers)

Adeva has joined Toptal; monitor the parent rather than a separate Adeva employer. Toptal’s core-team careers page explicitly permits fully remote work wherever, including halfway around the world, and lists internationally based opportunities. This is a worldwide work-location policy, not proof of Spanish payroll, work rights or eligibility for every role; the freelance talent network is separate.
