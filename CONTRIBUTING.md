# Contributing

Corrections, clearer country restrictions and new remote-friendly employers are welcome.

1. Link the official company homepage and stable careers page or official ATS board, not one expiring vacancy when a general board exists.
2. Add a short original factual business description. Do not paste marketing copy or another directory's descriptions.
3. Provide public evidence and its research date. Do not infer Spain eligibility from “remote” alone.
4. Mark mixed, restricted or uncertain cases clearly. Do not silently turn a historical role into a company-wide policy.
5. Never include names/emails of private contacts, applicant histories, negotiations, internal documents or authentication links.
6. Edit `data/companies.json`, run `python3 scripts/build.py`, and include the generated CSV/profiles/README in the pull request.

Do not delete a company solely because one role expired; update the caveat and source where appropriate.
