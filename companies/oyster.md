# Oyster

Oyster provides employer-of-record, global payroll and HR services that help businesses employ and support international teams.

- **Category:** HR technology
- **Website:** [Website](https://www.oysterhr.com/)
- **Jobs / careers:** [Careers](https://www.oysterhr.com/careers)
- **Historical remote signal:** Spain remote signal

Oyster's official careers page describes a fully remote, distributed team. Its linked Ashby board checked on 5 November 2026 lists an Accounts Payable Analyst role as remote in Spain, with additional locations in Romania, Hungary, Portugal, Poland and South Africa. The posting requires the employee to be based within UTC-3 to UTC+3 despite the broader work-from-anywhere messaging.

- **Historical record reviewed:** 2026-11-05
- **Public-source research date:** 2026-11-05

## Verification caveat

Employees must be authorized to work where they live. Country eligibility and timezone constraints are role-specific; Oyster's customer-facing employment coverage is not proof that its own team hires in every supported country. A talent-community signup is not a confirmed vacancy.
