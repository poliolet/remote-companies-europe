# Zapier

Zapier provides software connecting business applications into automated workflows and AI agents without requiring users to build custom integrations.

- **Category:** Workflow automation
- **Website:** [Website](https://zapier.com/)
- **Jobs / careers:** [Careers](https://zapier.com/jobs)
- **Historical remote signal:** Spain remote signal

Zapier is fully remote, but permanent hiring countries are governed by its eligibility sheet linked from current job descriptions. The sheet lists Spain, Ireland and the UK as Entity countries, while several other European countries are disallowed. This supports policy-level Spain monitoring, not a current Spain vacancy: reviewed openings are generally scoped elsewhere. Role-level region/time-zone limits and work rights still apply; annual summits involve in-person gatherings.
