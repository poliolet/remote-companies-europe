# itdesign

itdesign develops and implements CRM and project-portfolio-management software, including Meisterplan.

- **Category:** Enterprise software
- **Website:** [Website](https://itdesign.de/)
- **Jobs / careers:** [Careers](https://karriere.itdesign.de/jobs)
- **Historical remote signal:** Hybrid or country-restricted evidence

The old careers paths are stale; the current karriere.itdesign.de site explicitly says some jobs are 100% remote from anywhere in Germany. This is not every role and does not cover Spain. The workation perk is separate and does not authorise permanent foreign residence; the jobs page also accepts speculative interest.

- **Historical record reviewed:** 2026-11-11
- **Public-source research date:** 2026-11-11
