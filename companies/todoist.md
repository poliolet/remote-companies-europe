# Todoist

Todoist develops task management software that helps individuals and teams organize their work, track responsibilities, and plan everyday activities.

- **Category:** Productivity software
- **Website:** [Website](https://www.todoist.com/)

The current employer brand is Todoist (formerly Doist). Its careers page and indexed Apple Engineer posting explicitly allow work from anywhere in the world without location restrictions, supporting Spain at policy level rather than guaranteeing a Spanish payroll arrangement. Work is async-first; twice-yearly company retreats and a possible week co-working with an onboarding mentor involve travel. Compensation is location-dependent.
