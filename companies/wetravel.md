# WeTravel

WeTravel develops booking, itinerary, customer management and payment software for multi-day travel businesses.

- **Category:** Travel software and payments
- **Website:** [Website](https://www.wetravel.com/)
- **Jobs / careers:** [Careers](https://careers.wetravel.com/careers)
- **Historical remote signal:** Hybrid or country-restricted evidence

The official Senior Product Engineer - Lifecycle Team posting is Remote and lists Bulgaria, Estonia, Portugal, Italy, Hungary, Poland, Spain and Lithuania. The full description specifies a full-time fixed-term international B2B contractor agreement with monthly USD payments, not a confirmed local employee contract. It also describes designated-country remote work, an Amsterdam HQ program for 2-4 weeks annually and annual company offsites. The careers page describes team offsites and invitations to the Amsterdam program; mandatory attendance and travel-cost coverage are unspecified. Other roles are hybrid in Amsterdam, so this is role-specific evidence, not company-wide work-from-anywhere permission.
