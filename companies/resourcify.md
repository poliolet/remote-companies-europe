Resourcify develops software for waste management, recycling and circular-material operations, connecting business workflows with recovery partners and supporting evidence.

- **Category:** Circular economy and waste management software
- **Website:** [Website](https://www.resourcify.com/)

- **Historical remote signal:** Spain remote signal

Explicit permanent-employment eligibility for Spain appears on both the Teamtailor company policy and its fully remote engineering talent-community posting. The company policy lists fully remote Germany and Austria plus permanent employment in Spain and Portugal; the engineering talent-community posting instead lists Germany, Austria, Denmark, Italy and Spain. Treat Spain as confirmed, but resolve other country-list discrepancies with the employer. Teamtailor says staff are based within +/- 1 hour of CET for synchronous collaboration. German offices are optional under the policy. The current board has one concrete operations vacancy and four talent-community posts; its filter marks the operations vacancy as No Remote Work. Therefore this is a qualifying remote hiring policy/talent pipeline, not a verified active remote engineering vacancy. Offsites are annual in the main policy and twice yearly in the engineering talent post.
